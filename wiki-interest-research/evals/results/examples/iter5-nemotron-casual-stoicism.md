**Interest in stoicism: on Polish-language Wikipedia (pl) it is clearly declining (its share of that wiki's views changed -44.1% over the last 12 months vs the previous 12).**

- Polish-language Wikipedia (pl): interest is clearly declining. Its share of all Polish-language Wikipedia views changed -44.1% year over year (last 12 months vs the previous 12); raw views changed -50.0% while the whole wiki changed -8.8%. About 3,336 views per month on average over the last 12 months (about 6,667 in the previous 12); 17.7 per million views of that wiki.
  - Confidence High: 24 months of data; a solid audience (median 110 views per day); the month-to-month trend is statistically clear; the change is not driven by one-off spike days; views are spread over the year rather than a few days; no signs of automated traffic.
  - Most recent 3 months: share of views -8.5% vs the same months a year earlier (raw views: about 2,243 per month, vs about 2,524 in those months a year earlier); most of the change happened earlier in the year.

**What was measured and limits**
- Concept analysed for 'stoicism': Q48235: stoicism — school of Hellenistic philosophy who held that the practice of virtue suffices to attain eudaimonia.
- Each language is measured with one article (pl: Stoicyzm), which is a proxy for interest in the whole subject.
- These results describe readers of the Polish-language editions of Wikipedia, not countries; many people read English Wikipedia instead of their own language.
- Pageviews measure attention (curiosity, study, news), not willingness to pay.
