**What was measured and limits**
- Concept analysed for 'stoicism': school of Hellenistic philosophy who held that the practice of virtue suffices to attain eudaimonia.
- Each language is measured with one article (de: Stoa, en: Stoicism, pl: Stoicyzm, uk: Стоїцизм), which is a proxy for interest in the whole subject.
- These results describe readers of the German, English, Polish, Ukrainian-language editions of Wikipedia, not countries; many people read English Wikipedia instead of their own language.
- Pageviews measure attention (curiosity, study, news), not willingness to pay.
